# Using the AxomRank brand kit

## Choose the right logo

The preserved master is `logo/source/rang-ghar-original.jpg`, extracted without alteration from `AxomRank_Brand_Identity_Guide.docx`, `word/media/image1.jpg`. The SHA-256, original dimensions, trace bounds and method are in `logo/source/provenance.json`.

Use the detailed maroon mark on ivory/white at a width of at least 240 px. Use the small companion below that, preferably at 32 px or larger. At 16 px the favicon communicates the building silhouette; architectural detail cannot remain legible at that size. The profile and favicon have their own padded backgrounds. Transparent logo PNGs preserve alpha; the reversed mark needs a dark background.

Keep the source proportions. Do not stretch, rotate, replace roof forms or add generated architectural details. Clear space around a standalone mark is at least one quarter of its height. Treat the wordmark lockup as one unit; preserve its designed internal alignment. Leave at least one capital-letter height around a placed lockup.

The vector trace normalises the source to a single brand colour and removes tiny raster specks. The companion removes more small regions for readability, so it is a distinct source-derived variant. The detailed master remains the reference. Both need owner signoff as new derivatives before becoming the permanent identity authority.

## Wordmark and bilingual treatment

The name is **AxomRank**, with exact casing. The bilingual lockup adds the descriptor **অসমৰ জ্ঞান আৰু শিক্ষা** beneath the Latin name; it does not invent a replacement Assamese spelling of the brand. It means Assam's knowledge and education. The descriptor is proposed broader-brand copy, separate from the existing learning tagline.

Install the four Noto TTF files under `fonts/` to edit the live-text SVGs. Use outlined wordmarks when a printer or collaborator needs fixed appearance without font dependencies. Outlining preserves the appearance but turns text into shapes, so keep the live-text master for future copy edits.

The descriptor and sample copy have been checked for glyph coverage, shaping and layout here. No independent fluent Assamese reviewer has approved them. Record a fluent review before using the descriptor as final public brand wording.

## Colours and typography

Maroon `#80151A` is the main brand colour; ivory `#F7F2EC` the learning background; charcoal `#292929` the reading colour. Green `#3F6B4F` indicates a correct/completed state with an explicit label. Gold `#A77B37` is a decorative token; the supplied gold insignia retains its original raster colours. Do not use gold for small body text.

Use Noto Sans for English and Noto Sans Bengali for Assamese. Start at 38–40 px body text on 1080 px cards, with more line spacing for Assamese. Keep short lines. The stress-test card demonstrates long Assamese sentences and the distinct ৰ / ৱ characters. Recheck any edited copy: examples fitting now do not guarantee arbitrary replacement copy will fit.

## Social template families

1. Question: one question, four choices and a prompt to attempt first.
2. Explanation: explicit answer, two method steps and a short explanation.
3. Place: headline, credited photograph placeholder and one verified detail.
4. Opportunity: institution/programme, eligibility and deadline fields. It is visibly labelled a sample, not a live notice.
5. Correction: prior statement, corrected statement, reason, item ID and date.
6. Carousel start: headline and native 3D learning illustration.
7. Carousel end: one useful next action.
8. Vertical video: headline, media placeholder, two-line subtitle block and CTA.

Square cards are 1080 × 1080 with an 80 px safe inset. The vertical frame is 1080 × 1920 and leaves larger top/bottom buffers for interface overlays. These buffers are conservative template choices, not a guarantee for every platform/player. Preview against the actual publishing destination.

Open a live SVG in a vector editor. Edit text objects and individual shapes, then export at its native canvas size. Images are embedded, so downloaded templates do not depend on remote image URLs. Replace the place placeholder with a licensed photograph and the video area with actual video in your video editor. The SVG is a graphic frame, not an editable timeline or a rendered video.

For carousel series covered by the later preferences, preserve the supplied gold insignia. Do not add woven edges or gamosa borders. Keep verification records internally; sources omitted from finished cards/captions still belong in the source pack.

## Editable source versus preview

SVG is the editable design source. PNG is the ready-to-view raster export. ICO is a browser icon container. JSON and CSS are developer references. A generated image is decorative learning artwork, not proof of a student, an institution or a live feature.

The gallery supports local browsing and downloads; it does not replace a design editor. Review answers, facts, permissions, final wording and destination links before publication. The original source guides and later explicit decisions retain their authority until the owner adopts this derivative kit.
