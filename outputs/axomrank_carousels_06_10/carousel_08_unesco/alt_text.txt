slide_03.png: Square ivory, maroon and gold 3D study-journal card with the golden winged insignia. Manas — natural heritage UNESCO lists Manas Wildlife Sanctuary, inscribed in 1985. It lies in the Himalayan foothills. 

slide_04.png: Square ivory, maroon and gold 3D study-journal card with the golden winged insignia. Moidams — cultural heritage The Moidams at Charaideo were inscribed in 2024. They represent Tai-Ahom funerary traditions.
