## Reusable AI commission briefs

**Learning scene:** Create an editorial 3D illustration of an adult learner reviewing one question on a phone at an ordinary study desk in Assam. Natural proportions and plausible setting. Warm ivory and restrained maroon accents, clear space for a separate text overlay, square composition. Do not invent readable interface screens, scores or testimonials. No textile border, gamosa framing, logos or watermarks. Describe the region and context in the brief and have local details reviewed.

**Concept diagram:** Illustrate a percentage change using clearly separable groups of objects. Provide the intended count and method to the designer; verify every quantity manually. Leave all labels and numbers for editable typography. Ivory background, maroon highlights, square composition, no decorative objects that confuse the explanation.

**History scene:** Begin from a written evidence brief listing period, place, supported material details and disputed elements. Produce a clearly modern educational reconstruction. Avoid precise claims that the evidence does not support. Keep a caption stating the reconstruction status in the internal asset metadata and wherever the image could be mistaken for documentary proof.

## Definition of complete

An asset is complete when its editable original, export, caption/alt text, source or generation record, rights/credit, reviewer, approval state and intended uses are stored together. A collection is complete when its scope and gaps are explicit. A repo with many attractive images but no source files, rights or update process is still unfinished.
