# AxomRank brand foundation and expansion

Prepared 11 October 2026. This is a proposed expansion brief based on the owner's stated vision and existing repository documents. It does not claim that the expanded services have launched.

## What the brand is today

AxomRank is currently a learning app. The October brand guide describes it as Assam's exam practice companion, serving ADRE and other Assam exam aspirants through questions, understandable explanations, regular practice and Assamese/English access. The application repository README describes successive learning-platform versions; its feature and question counts are repository claims, not a live product audit.

The useful habit is attempt → understand → review → choose the next task. Preserve that focus in education campaigns. Never imply guaranteed selection, a particular rank, or capabilities that have not been demonstrated in the live app.

## What the brand should become

The owner's ambition is a single trusted starting point for information about Assam. Proposed positioning: **AxomRank helps people learn about Assam and find reliable information for their next decision.** The learning app is the first useful service within this broader identity.

A complete brand needs a dependable information system as well as a visual library: factual records, update dates, source references, accessible language, local contributors, corrections and clear routes to official services. “One stop” describes ease of discovery; it should not promise that every fact is available or permanently current.

## Audience and value

| Audience | Immediate need | Useful destination |
|---|---|---|
| Exam aspirants | Understand and practise | Existing learning app and verified topic lessons |
| Students and families | Compare education options | Pilot college/programme records with admission-source links |
| Residents | Find official information | Plain-language explainers and direct official links |
| Travellers and diaspora | Understand places and culture | Reviewed place guides, maps and credited photographs |
| Educators and local contributors | Share accurate knowledge | Research registers and a reviewed submission process |

Start with aspirants and students. Broaden coverage only as editors and update capacity become available. Include the Brahmaputra Valley, Barak Valley and hill areas; do not treat one community, language or heritage period as the whole of Assam.

## Brand architecture

Use **AxomRank** as the parent name. Proposed navigation labels are Learn, Opportunities, Places, Culture and Everyday Assam. These are proposed sections, not new launched brands or separate accounts. History publications remain a content series under AxomRank. Keep the selected book title **From Sukaphaa to Gomdhar Konwar**.

Keep **Prepare Smarter. Rank Higher.** for the learning product. Retain **Rooted in Assam. Rising with You.** as the existing emotional signature where appropriate. Broader information pages should describe the information they provide, rather than carry an exam promise.

The guide proposes axomrank.org as the public front door and references angsumi.online/ADRE as the learning destination. Verify ownership, availability and redirects before changing public links; neither was audited here. Preserve working access during any domain transition.

## Visual authority and precedence

Read the original [identity guide](../../AxomRank_Brand_Identity_Guide.docx), [Instagram strategy](../../AxomRank_Instagram_Content_Strategy_for_Assam.docx) and [later carousel decisions](../../CAROUSEL_PREFERENCES.md) together. Later explicit carousel preferences take precedence where they conflict with the original guide.

- Deep maroon `#80151A`, warm ivory `#F7F2EC`, charcoal `#292929`, white `#FFFFFF`; muted green `#3F6B4F` for correct/completed states, always paired with words or symbols.
- Preserve the supplied Rang Ghar artwork and proportions. Develop simplified vector variants from that actual source with a designer; do not generate an imitation.
- Use the supplied golden left-facing winged insignia for the applicable newer carousel series. Preserve its silhouette. It is a brand motif, not an official government emblem.
- Current finished carousel cards are square 1:1. The old 1080 × 1350 recommendation is superseded for this series. Use aesthetic 3D illustration when producing these cards. No gamosa, woven borders or textile motifs in the current visual identity.
- English and Assamese must both contain the full meaning. Noto Sans / Noto Sans Bengali are starting font candidates from the guide; proofread Assamese glyphs, spelling and line breaks with a fluent reader. Do not bake substantive text into generated background art when an editable text layer can preserve accuracy.
- Keep source and verification records internally. The later carousel instructions omit source labels from finished cards/captions; this does not remove the evidence requirement. Information pages and directory records should expose source links and update dates.

## Voice and public wording

Sound like a capable local teacher: direct, useful, respectful and precise. Identify uncertainty. Avoid rumours, hostile regional comparisons, invented testimonials or shaming low scores.

Current short description: “AxomRank helps Assam's learners practise, understand mistakes and build their knowledge.”

Proposed broader description, for use as coverage becomes available: “Learn about Assam. Explore its education, places, culture and everyday information with AxomRank.”

Do not advertise a complete statewide directory or news service before coverage and an update process exist.
