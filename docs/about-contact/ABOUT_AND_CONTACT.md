# AxomRank about and contact copy

## About AxomRank

AxomRank is a learning app for Assam's learners and exam aspirants. It brings learning and practice into a locally rooted experience, with clear explanations and attention to Assamese and English communication.

Our wider ambition is to make AxomRank a trusted starting point for information about Assam—from education and opportunities to its places, history, culture and everyday information. The learning app is our starting point; additional information collections will grow as we develop and review them.

We aim to make information useful and understandable, check important facts, and explain corrections clearly.
