# AxomRank frequently asked questions

## What is AxomRank?

AxomRank is currently a learning app for Assam's learners and exam aspirants. Its wider goal is to become a trusted starting point for information about Assam.

## Who runs AxomRank?

AxomRank is run as a sole proprietorship by Angsuman Das. He is the named proprietor and contact for the brand.

## Is every planned information service available now?

The learning app is the current starting point. Education directories, place guides and other information collections are development plans unless a specific collection has been published and identified as available.

## How do I report a factual error?

Email the relevant page/post, the claim you believe needs correction, and a supporting source. AxomRank's editorial workflow calls for checking the evidence and updating affected versions.

## Does AxomRank guarantee an exam rank or selection?

No. Learning and practice support preparation; a tagline is not a selection guarantee.

## Are the historical illustrations photographs?

Generated historical illustrations are modern illustrations or reconstructions. They do not authenticate a person's likeness, an event or an exact architectural appearance. Teaching facts are checked separately.

## Can I reuse a picture or document from the repository?

Check the individual asset's rights record. Public access to a file does not establish reuse permission. If its status is pending, request permission before publishing it. Some assets, such as the bundled Noto fonts, have specific licences that must be followed.

## Can I contribute a photograph, story or recording?

Yes—contact Angsuman with a description of your contribution and its intended use. Before publication, agree on the specific asset, permitted channels, credit, term and any payment, and record any necessary permission from people appearing in it.

## How do I check current prices or available features?

Use the current product page or contact Angsuman. This kit does not assert a price, paid plan or feature beyond the current learning-app description.
